## v0.17.0 - 2026-10-23
### Added
* `rollback` and `traffic --to` bring a service's riders — its Container Apps jobs and `ingress: false` workers — to the version the side the traffic moved to holds, after the traffic has moved. Nothing is recorded for it: a worker is restored from its newest revision on that version, a job from its newest run on it, and the image tag alone where neither is left. A rider that fails is named with the `apply --set` that finishes the job and fails the command without moving the traffic back; one the platform cannot restore is listed as not moved. `traffic` shows each rider's version under its service.
### Fixed
* A failed switch no longer reverts a rider whose own write failed, which had already put itself back; a worker paid a second ready wait for it.
