## v0.15.2 - 2026-10-22
### Fixed
* Stage a blue-green Container App revision that scales to zero. A revision that is Healthy at zero replicas now counts as staged, and the smoke test that follows is what wakes it. Before, staging waited for a replica nothing would ever start.
* Give up on an Azure write that is still in progress after five minutes, and say so, instead of waiting until CI kills the job. Container Apps refuses every later write to that app with ContainerAppOperationInProgress until the stuck one finishes.
